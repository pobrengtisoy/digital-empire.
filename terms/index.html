Terms of sale

What you are buying

When you buy an RZ product, RZ Digital Empire sets it up for your business, based on the demo you see on our site, and keeps it online for you. The price and what is included are shown on the Buy page before you pay.

Payment

You pay by QR Ph (Maya, GCash or any bank app) and send us your payment reference number. We check every payment by hand. Setup starts once your payment is confirmed.

Setup time: 1 to 24 hours

Your product is ready within 1 to 24 hours after we have both your confirmed payment and the details we need from you (for example your logo, photos, menu items and prices). If we are waiting for something from you, the clock starts when we receive it.

We build what you want

  • We only work on what you ask for: your business name, logo, colours, photos, items, prices and other details the product supports.
  • During setup, we keep adjusting until you are happy with it, within the features of the product you bought.
  • Nothing goes live without your OK.
  • Bigger extras that the product does not have yet can be quoted separately. We will always tell you the price first.

We solve the issues

If something does not work as shown, message us on Telegram and we fix it at no extra cost. This covers every month you stay subscribed, and for 30 days after the event or season for RZ Invite and RZ Liga.

Monthly fee

For products with a monthly fee, the first month is included in your first payment. After that, the fee is due on the same date each month. To stop, tell us before your next due date and we will not charge you again. If a month is not paid within 7 days of its due date, your product goes offline. We keep your data for 30 days after that, then delete it.

Your content

Your logo, photos, menu and other content stay yours. You confirm you have the right to use them, and that your product will not be used for anything illegal or misleading.

Staying online

Your product runs on Cloudflare’s network. We work to keep it online at all times, but we cannot promise it will never go down. If it does, we fix it as fast as we can.

Refunds

All payments are final. We do not give refunds. Instead, we keep working on your product until it works the way you asked and the way the demo shows.

Two exceptions, to be fair to you:

  • If we cannot deliver the product you paid for at all, we return your full payment.
  • If you paid more than the amount shown, or paid twice by mistake, we return the extra.

Months already paid are not refunded when you stop, but you keep your product until the end of that month.

Privacy

We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, business name, mobile number, email (if you give it), payment reference number and any notes you write.
  • When you have an owner account: your email, your password (stored only as a scrambled hash we cannot read), and your shop details and menu.
  • When your customers order from your RZ Menu: the name, contact number, address and order they enter. This belongs to your business. We only store it for you.

Why we use it

Only to check your payment, set up your product, keep it running, and contact you about it. We do not sell your details, show you ads, or share them with anyone, unless the law requires us to.

Where it is kept

On Cloudflare’s servers, sent over secure (HTTPS) connections. Only RZ can see purchase records. Each shop owner can only see their own shop’s orders.

Cookies

We use one cookie, only to keep you logged in to your dashboard. No tracking or advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept for as long as Philippine tax rules require. Owner accounts are kept until you delete them. You can delete your account any time in Dashboard, Settings, Delete my account. This removes your shop, menu and orders for good.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or ask us to stop using them. Message us on Telegram at @BRZBSST and we will reply within 7 days.

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Changes never apply to a payment you already made.
