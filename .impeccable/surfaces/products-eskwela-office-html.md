# RZ Eskwela — school office console (and its public school page and parent portal)

## Scope and mode
Primary: `products/eskwela/office.html`, Operate. Related: `products/eskwela/index.html` (school page + online enrolment, Persuade-light inside the same world) and `products/eskwela/parent.html` (parent portal, Read). Audience: registrar, cashier, principal and teachers of small Philippine private schools, tutorial and review centres; parents on phones. Job: enrol, bill instalments, collect, grade, release report cards, take attendance (Full plan), post announcements (Full plan). Two plans only: Plus and Full. User asked for "very clean, very formal".

## Direction contract
THESIS: The school office run with commencement formality. Refuses the category default of a grey dashboard with a blue accent and icon tiles; refuses chalkboards and crayons.
OWN-WORLD: Academic-black rail and header ink (#15171d family) over white work paper; regalia gold reserved for the primary action and the active item; each grade level owns a hood colour (maroon, emerald, sapphire, violet, saffron, slate, crimson, teal) that follows that level everywhere. Engraved inscriptional capitals (Cinzel) only for the school's name, report-card titles and seal; Source Sans 3 for all working text; tabular figures for money and grades. Hairline rules, no shadows on content, no cards-in-cards.
STORY: The office sees who owes, who is late, who applied, and acts in one click; parents see the same truth on a private link.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail: school seal monogram and name, sections (Today, Students, Fees & payments, Grades, Attendance, Announcements, School settings). Main: four ledger figures (enrolled, collected this month, receivable, late accounts), then two work lists side by side: applicants awaiting approval and late accounts with reminder actions. Gold primary "Approve" and "Record payment".
FORM: Regalia, assigned candidate 5 of my grounded list (diploma, report card, uniform patch, yearbook, regalia, gate lettering, school ID); seed: 8cd7f7ed1be63a4c. Signature move: the hood-colour band — a level's colour drawn as a narrow V-shaped hood mark beside every level name and as the report card's header band and the parent portal's top band. Raise from declined jet-age ticket wallet: paid, late and void are stamped states that never delete. Raise from declined dark console: destructive actions isolated and outline-only until confirmed.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints
Strict CSP (no inline styles/scripts), no HTML strings from user text, phone-first for the parent portal, server enforces plans, student data scoped per school (Data Privacy Act). Report cards are the school's own; never claim official DepEd forms.
