# Surface brief: marketplace homepage (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Replacement world for the whole RZ Digital Empire; this surface sets it.

Audience & job: Filipino small businesses, freelancers/creators and agencies on a phone, deciding in seconds whether RZ products can carry *their* brand. Action: type your business name and see it printed on working RZ products, then open a working demo (RZ Form first) or copy a custom-project brief.
Proof: 4 working demos (Ledger, Arena, Insight, Form), 5 planned concepts labelled planned. No prices, customers, testimonials or checkout exist; never invent them.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, strict CSP (no inline styles), no HTML-string insertion of user text, phone-first, Philippines/English/₱.

## Direction contract

THESIS: RZ is the neighbourhood print shop for software: you bring your name, we print it on working tools. The page refuses the category default of a dark SaaS hero with device mock-ups, and also the previous lime-on-charcoal editorial brutalism.

OWN-WORLD: Glossy vinyl tarpaulin in full-strength process colour (cyan, magenta, yellow, key black) as whole-region fields, brass grommets at tarp corners, print-shop proof sheets on white stock with crop marks, registration targets and CMYK colour bars, numbered order slips. Type is loud, wide, celebratory tarp lettering for headlines and a plain workhorse sans for reading text.

STORY: The visitor sees their own business name printed huge, understands that every RZ product can wear it, tries a working demo, and leaves a brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width tarp (grommets at the corners) is the hero ground; on it a giant headline that is the visitor's business name (sample "Kape ni Lola" until they type), a one-line offer, and a working name field with a "Print my proof" action. The top bar is set on the tarp as plain links: RZ mark, Products, How it works, Start a project.

FORM: Filipino tarpaulin print shop, candidate 1 of my ordered list (chosen by the user over the rolled candidate 7, "Registered"). seed:2d1200b3aa7fdfee

Signature interaction: "Print my proof" sweeps a print head across the tarp, laying the typed name into the hero and into every product proof below it.
Motion grammar: print-head sweeps and paper slides; nothing floats or glows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
